# FoxsMusic profile appearance

The profile appearance controls are available in Settings.

- **Style:** Classic, Neon, or Glass.
- **Seasonal mode:** Normal, Halloween, or Christmas.
- **Custom accent:** choose a color using the color picker. Common UI accents, borders, highlights, buttons, and visualizer colors follow the chosen color.
- **Gradient:** Crimson, Ember, Violet, Ocean, or Monochrome.
- **Reset:** restores the classic profile, Normal mode, Crimson gradient, and default crimson accent without clearing the music library or audio settings.

## Seasonal scenes

- **Normal:** the regular FoxsMusic interface without seasonal decorations.
- **Halloween:** cobwebs and spiders hang from the hero-card corners; bats glide near the sides; a softly glowing smiling pumpkin silhouette sits in the middle. Purple and ember lighting supports the scene.
- **Christmas:** animated snow falls across the hero and subtle snow specks move behind the main library; frosted ice crystals line the banner and panel edges; garland and ornaments twinkle above a decorated tree beside a glowing fireplace.

Visual effects are enabled by default. Turning them off leaves the selected seasonal artwork visible but stops its animations.
